# Section 2 · Motor Insurance

**Clause 2.1 — What motor cover includes.**
Accidental collision damage, fire, theft of the insured vehicle, and third-party liability for injury or property damage caused by the insured vehicle. Comprehensive policies cover own-damage; third-party-only policies do not.

**Clause 2.2 — Third-party-only policies.**
A third-party-only policy covers damage the insured vehicle causes to others. It does **not** cover damage to the insured's own vehicle. An own-damage claim on a third-party-only policy is rejected under this clause.

**Clause 2.3 — Wear, tear, and mechanical breakdown.**
Gradual wear, tyre wear, brake-pad wear, corrosion, and mechanical or electrical breakdown that is not the result of a sudden external accident are **excluded**. A worn-clutch or dead-battery claim is not a collision and is rejected.

**Clause 2.4 — Driver eligibility.**
The driver at the time of loss must hold a valid licence for the vehicle class. A claim where the driver was unlicensed, or whose licence had expired, is excluded.

**Clause 2.5 — Driving under the influence.**
Any loss occurring while the driver was under the influence of alcohol or unprescribed drugs is excluded, and the claim is rejected.

**Clause 2.6 — Racing and unlawful use.**
Loss during racing, speed testing, off-road competition, or while the vehicle was used for a purpose not permitted by the policy (e.g., private car used as a taxi without a commercial rider) is excluded.

**Clause 2.7 — Windscreen and glass.**
Standalone windscreen or window-glass damage is covered up to a sub-limit of EGP 3,000 per policy year without affecting the no-claims record.

**Clause 2.8 — Motor deductible.**
A comprehensive motor claim carries the deductible stated on the policy. The deductible is subtracted from the approved amount.

**Clause 2.9 — Motor waiting period.**
None for accidents. Theft cover begins 7 days after policy start.

**Clause 2.10 — Required documents (motor).**
Police report for theft or third-party accident; photographs of the damage; a repair estimate or garage invoice; a copy of the driver's licence and the vehicle registration. A theft claim also requires the police theft report and the vehicle's spare key.
