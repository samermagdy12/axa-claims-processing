# Section 4 · Travel Insurance

**Clause 4.1 — What travel cover includes.**
Emergency medical treatment abroad, trip cancellation for a covered reason, lost or delayed baggage, and travel-document replacement, for the duration of a single trip or an annual multi-trip policy.

**Clause 4.2 — Emergency medical abroad.**
Sudden illness or accidental injury occurring during the trip is covered up to the medical sub-limit. Treatment for a pre-existing condition abroad follows the same 12-month rule as Clause 1.3.

**Clause 4.3 — Trip cancellation (covered reasons).**
Cancellation is covered only for: the traveller's own serious illness, the death or hospitalisation of an immediate family member, jury service, or a government travel ban to the destination. Cancellation because the traveller changed their mind, or for a fear of travel without a formal ban, is excluded.

**Clause 4.4 — Baggage.**
Lost, stolen, or damaged baggage is covered up to EGP 8,000, with a single-item limit of EGP 2,500. A property irregularity report from the airline or a police report is required. Cash and travel documents are covered only up to EGP 1,500.

**Clause 4.5 — Baggage delay vs baggage loss.**
Baggage **delayed** by more than 6 hours pays a fixed inconvenience benefit of EGP 1,000 against receipts for essentials. Baggage **permanently lost** is settled under Clause 4.4. The two are different claim types and must not be paid twice for the same bag.

**Clause 4.6 — High-risk activities.**
Injuries from professional sport, motor racing, mountaineering above 3,000 m, or scuba diving below 30 m are excluded unless the Adventure rider is held.

**Clause 4.7 — Travel waiting period.**
None. Cover begins on the trip start date shown on the policy.

**Clause 4.8 — Required documents (travel).**
For medical: the treating physician's report and itemised invoices. For cancellation: proof of the covered reason (medical certificate, death certificate, official notice). For baggage: the airline property irregularity report or police report, plus receipts or proof of ownership.
