# Section 5 · Universal Exclusions

These exclusions apply to **every** policy line and override any cover granted elsewhere in this handbook.

**Clause 5.1 — War and terrorism.** Loss caused by war, invasion, civil war, insurrection, or an act of terrorism is excluded across all lines.

**Clause 5.2 — Nuclear and radioactive.** Loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination is excluded.

**Clause 5.3 — Intentional acts.** Loss deliberately caused by the insured, or arising from the insured's own criminal act, is excluded.

**Clause 5.4 — Illegal activity.** Loss occurring while the insured was committing an illegal act is excluded.

**Clause 5.5 — Sanctions.** No cover is provided where doing so would breach applicable sanctions or law.

**Clause 5.6 — Consequential loss.** Indirect or consequential financial loss (loss of income, loss of value, inconvenience) is excluded unless a clause expressly provides a benefit for it.
